HOW IT WORKS

From grocery sales to smarter stock.

A simple process for making more informed restocking decisions, while remembering that real demand can change.

STEP 01

Record recent sales

Start with how many units of each grocery item sold during the last week.

STEP 02

Check stock on hand

Count what is available and remove expired or damaged items from usable stock.

STEP 03

Adjust expected demand

Account for busy days, local events, weather or seasonal shopping changes.

STEP 04

Plan a restock

Use the suggested quantity as a starting point, then check expiry and delivery timing.

RESPONSIBLE GROCERY RETAIL

Keep shelves ready. Reduce avoidable waste.

DemandIQ focuses on grocery stock planning: ordering closer to what customers need can help stores avoid overstocking, protect freshness and use resources more carefully.

🥬

Reduce food waste

Fresh foods such as produce, dairy and bakery items can spoil quickly. Smaller, better-timed orders may help reduce items that go unsold before expiry.

📦

Improve availability

Tracking sales and stock helps store teams spot popular essentials and avoid running out of products customers need.

🚚

Plan deliveries better

Considering supplier lead time and delivery schedules can support more practical ordering and fewer last-minute restocks.

🌍

Support responsible consumption

Careful inventory planning aligns with SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production, especially the goal of reducing food loss and waste.
